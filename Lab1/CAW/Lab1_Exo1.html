Brief History

Palestine has a long history that dates back to ancient civilizations. It was part of various empires throughout history, including the Roman and Byzantine periods.

During the modern era, the region went through Ottoman rule and later the British Mandate. The 20th century saw major political changes and conflicts that shaped the present-day situation.

Today, the history of Palestine remains central to regional politics; many international efforts continue to seek a peaceful and just solution.
