Since October 7, 2023, the Gaza Strip has faced continuous bombardments resulting in the killing of over 40,000 martyrs, including more than 15,000 children and 9,000 women. Tens of thousands have been injured, and thousands remain missing under the rubble.

Infrastructure damage is catastrophic: more than 70% of homes have been destroyed or severely damaged, while schools, hospitals, mosques, and churches have also been targeted. According to official sources such as Sehatty and Al Jazeera, Gaza is now facing famine, disease, and lack of medical aid.

The humanitarian situation remains tragic with millions displaced and entire neighborhoods turned to ashes.
